# FDE knowledge review

Read [versioned update contract](references/update-contract.md). Deliver a source delta and a proposed version change that can be reviewed independently of vendor popularity.

Check first-party content and its date, fixed version and limitations. Distinguish employer demand, vendor observation, paper benchmark, platform recommendation and your own run evidence. Social metadata is a lead rather than proof that the video/article was read.

Group matching stories before summarization; derive topics from reviewed source metadata. Compare changes against representative and holdout tasks, state compatibility and rollback, then submit a candidate version with changelog and hashes. Human review decides company adoption within the measured scope.

For this radar, FDE and Android share one 14-day due clock and durable financial ledger, at USD 0.50/batch and USD 2/month by default. Preserve saved paid requests and ambiguous outcomes. Changes to budgets or external services use the user's authorized scope and concrete configuration.
